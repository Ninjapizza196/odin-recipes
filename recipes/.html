Tomato Basil Penne Pasta

Description

This tomato basil penne pasta is a Mediterranean-style family staple, with tender noodles in a creamy, cheesy sauce and a bright, herby finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 (8 ounce) package penne pasta 1 tablespoon basil oil 1 tablespoon olive oil 3 cloves garlic, minced 1 pint grape tomatoes, halved 1 cup shredded pepper Jack cheese 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese 1 tablespoon minced fresh basil
